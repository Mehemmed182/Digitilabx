Website SEO Fundamentals for Beginners

Search Engine Optimization does not require mysterious tricks. It requires clean semantic code, rapid page speeds, descriptive metadata, and authentic answers to human search queries.

Share article: WhatsApp LinkedIn

Search engine algorithms have evolved radically. Gone are the days when stuffing hidden keywords at the bottom of a web page could manipulate rankings. In 2026, Google, Bing, and AI search engines (like Perplexity and ChatGPT Search) index websites based on clarity, performance, author credibility, and user satisfaction.

1. The Four Foundational Pillars of On-Page SEO

A. Title Tags & Meta Descriptions

Your <title> tag is the single most critical on-page signal. Keep it under 60 characters and ensure your main topic is front-loaded: 

<title>Website Design Services in Baku — DigitilabX</title>
<meta name="description" content="Custom, fast, multilingual websites built for businesses in Azerbaijan.">

B. Proper Heading Hierarchy

Always maintain a logical structure: exactly one <h1> per document representing the main topic, followed by <h2> for major sub-themes, and <h3> for supporting points. Never skip heading levels for styling purposes.

C. Mobile Responsiveness & Core Web Vitals

Google indexes pages using its mobile smartphone crawler. If your layout shifts unexpectedly or takes longer than 2.5 seconds to render on a standard mobile connection, your rankings will suffer.

D. Structured Data (Schema.org)

Adding JSON-LD or microdata (such as itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/Article") helps search engines parse publication dates, authors, and headlines directly for rich snippets.

2. Technical SEO for Free Static Sites

If you are hosting your website on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages, you already enjoy major SEO benefits:

  • Automated Canonical URLs: Add <link rel="canonical" href="https://..."> to prevent duplicate content flags.
  • Hreflang Tags: For multilingual sites in English, Azerbaijani, Russian, and Turkish, declare alternate language versions so search engines serve the right page to the right country.
  • XML Sitemap & Robots.txt: Maintain a sitemap.xml listing all active URLs and submit it directly inside Google Search Console.
"Good SEO is simply good design made visible to machines. If human visitors love reading your content and find answers quickly, search engines will reward you."
